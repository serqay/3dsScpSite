Welcome, Researcher!

NEW! Containment Breach now runs on a Nintendo 3DS. This is a fan-made port of the classic SCP horror game: the whole facility, the monsters and the endings, on two screens.

Wake up in the cell, follow the guards, and then survive. Light Containment, Heavy Containment, Entrance Zone, and the exit.

>> Go to the download page <<

What is in the port?

  • The full facility, generated randomly for every new game
  • The intro with the escort, the SCP-173 chamber and the alarm
  • SCP-173, 106, 096, 049, 939, 966, 860-2, 513-1, 1048-A and more, plus the MTF units and the Apache
  • Items, inventory, SCP-294, SCP-914, the radio and the S-NAV on the bottom screen
  • Endings Gate A and Gate B
  • English and Russian text and voices, subtitles
  • Hardware-decoded audio, three save slots, an Old 3DS profile and a New 3DS profile (with stereoscopic 3D)

Dimension 1499 and the pocket dimension are in too. (hover to declassify... or not)
